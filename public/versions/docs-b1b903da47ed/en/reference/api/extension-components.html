Note

Every callback is synchronous, bounded and non-reentrant. claim grants the unique role owner of this exact lane and atomically rejects a reused/non-increasing local boot epoch, even after ordinary join. The context binds the exact physical lane and peer execution epoch covered by stopped. An epoch observation, cancel, deadline or elapsed timeout never supplies this proof. No existing single-core SoC supplies it.

Acquire the selected F407 SPI2 personality and fixed pads.

The caller owns the external master and holds CK idle LOW and WS HIGH continuously from before initialization/submission until acquire-observed ACTIVE. Pin reads sample instants; they cannot prove electrical silence. The master transmits exactly the declared complete frames and stops before a missing next word. Restart begins at the left channel. A prefilling cancellation with CK idle cannot empty DR; preserve QUARANTINED storage until coordinated drain clocks allow TXE then fresh BSY observations. This error drain can underrun and does not promise valid stereo audio or a complete transmitted frame; errors report zero transferred bytes. Timeout, TC or NDTR0 never proves wire completion or final access. The caller also supplies valid operating voltage, I/O levels, load, CK/WS/SD timing and DMA refill margin under DS8626. ACTIVE and pin samples do not measure or qualify those electrical/rate conditions.

One privileged unmasked task serializes every method and external owner lifecycle. Preserve incoming masks on all rejection paths. NMI/HardFault, other DMA/IRQ/pin owners, reset/reclock/debugger writers and concurrent Platform stop are excluded. Observed reset or clock-profile history loss while borrowed permanently retains the request; restoring reset-looking registers cannot establish prior final access. Main SRAM is DMA-visible; CCM is not. Chip pin capability establishes neither a PCB route nor codec/electrical HIL. The assembly and fixture supply the actual F407/package identity: shared device-group and density reads alone do not distinguish an F405 or authenticate the physical package.

Note

Exact F407VE/ZG, baremetal, HSE8/PLL168 and live TIM2 only. The normal hardware role exclusively claims SPI2/I2S2, PB12 WS/PB13 CK/PB15 SD AF5, DMA1 Stream4 channel0 and both vectors. No PLLI2S, continuous DBM or IRQ service is enabled. Peripheral/pad/SPI2 gate ownership lasts until stop SUCCESS, independently of each request’s shorter frame loan. The selected cold-reset Platform separately holds the shared GPIOB/DMA1 gates. Initialization requires both already on; this provider never enables or withdraws them. Every shared domain user, including external HAL users, must join before Platform stop. Clock-gating is not proof of a reset engine; unknown running-state attachment is unsupported.
