Detailed Description

Stable caller copies, with independently known fact groups.

Note
request/stream may be NULL; their presence establishes the corresponding group. known accepts only LIFECYCLE, OWNER, SERVICE and OBSERVERS. Zero-initialized unknown values never establish readiness, health or a missed service. OWNER fields may come from an owner observer only when its diagnostic_result succeeded. They describe admission and pending work, not the cause of a BUSY service result. SERVICE timestamps use the same monotonic domain; both are finite and maximum_service_gap_us is a finite positive declared budget. This helper reads no clock itself. Copies remain stable throughout this call. Observations need not be simultaneous across devices.
