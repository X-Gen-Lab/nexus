Cold construct one exact copied mediation over a real owner.

Parameters
[out]mediationFresh zero privileged storage, initialized once.
[in,out]slotsExact caller-owned cells/payloads, no maximum pool.
[in]countPositive cell count representable in a scalar slot ID.
[in]configImmutable trusted principal/grant/copy/owner binding.
Returns
SUCCESS or INVALID/STATE/CONTEXT/BUSY/EXHAUSTED, or trusted claim error without new retained users or hardware effects; every rejected output remains unchanged.
Note
Privileged unmasked Thread with owner idle and no concurrent users. Cold O(n squared) alias checks reject all known mutable cell/payload/control overlaps and user-RAM aliases. Admission checks fixed region extents and copies at most the selected exact payload budget; no queue or worker is added. Keep policy, cells, owner, provider and all contexts stable through permanent close, consumption and successful join. Pure cold checks precede a constructing reservation and the trusted role claim; no fallible step follows successful claim. Fresh objects require a larger externally claimed session; this object cannot be reconstructed to reset its epochs.
