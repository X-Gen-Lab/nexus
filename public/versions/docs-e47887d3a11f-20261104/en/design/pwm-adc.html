Finite PWM and ADC windows with one hardware owner¶

Use this contract to select and execute a finite, timer-triggered acquisition window through nx_pwm_adc_t. The current implementation is the exact STM32G474RET6 finite-regular-dma software reference described below. It keeps one PWM duty frame constant while DMA fills a finite raw sample array, then withdraws the sources before releasing the request. Product control algorithms, power-stage policy and inspected PCB wiring remain external. HIL is not_executed.

Select one coupled interface¶

FIG-16 describes current shared mechanisms, not a measured PWM/ADC timing diagram. In this selection, its generated face is nx_pwm_adc_t, its provider is the joint TIM1/ADC1 owner, and its application-owned storage is the request, duty array and sample array. The arrows retain their generation, lookup and call meanings. Factory lookup never primes ADC, starts PWM or schedules work.

The normal input is the maintained TOML fixture. Its software-only Board package supplies silicon pin and voltage assumptions; it does not establish a fitted analog circuit. The strict resolver selects a single Construction, a compact NX_PWM_ADC_ID_WINDOW and nx_factory_pwm_adc. It rejects conflicting TIM1, ADC/common, comparator, DMA, vector and pad claims before publishing an atomic bundle. Ordinary PWM-group and ADC faces do not independently expose this selection’s hardware.

Prepare, admit and explicitly advance¶

  1. Retrieve the immutable generated face and explicitly start Platform. Startup prepares the cold owner; it starts neither a request nor PWM output.

  2. Initialize the request once, then prepare a separate sample array and complete three-channel duty frame with a finite absolute monotonic deadline. Preparation copies frame metadata, borrows nothing and touches no samples.

  3. Submit from the unique privileged, unmasked task executor. A rejected call leaves no new effects or retained references. NX_SUCCESS means ACCEPTED, including an arm error that may already have settled through its real drain.

  4. After acceptance, preserve request and duty storage and exclude application reads/writes of sample storage. IRQs latch bounded facts; the same task calls service to advance completion or withdrawal. No implicit task supplies this progress.

  5. Acquire-observe NX_REQUEST_SETTLED before consuming result and samples. Join adapter/observer references separately before preparing the storage for another explicit request. Ordinary successful completion never rearms itself.

Preparation rejects overlapping, misaligned or wrapping request, frame, duty and sample spans without changing the initialized request. The provider additionally rejects aliases with its retained state, facts, memory-domain descriptors and known MMIO controls before admission. Known control descriptors are rejected before their fields are read.

Each accepted window loads a coherent frame while TIM1 is stopped and reloads preloads behind a quiet trigger route. It enables ADC and discards two software conversions before enabling normal DMA and the external regular trigger. Neither prime enters user DMA storage. The finite prime sequence uses at most a 100 us deadline and a secondary poll bound. Each later explicit window repeats ADC enable and both primes; idle never leaves the BULB sampler enabled.

The full-window deadline is an observation rule. Without a trusted completion timestamp, TC first observed at or after that deadline reports TIMEOUT. A TC already observed before the deadline may complete its required withdrawal later. This does not establish a physical execution-time bound. Timeout and cancellation retain the same loan until real source drain succeeds.

Prove withdrawal before publishing a result¶

FIG-06 supplies the shared request-state contract. ACCEPTED and REJECTED are admission results, not additional request states. In this provider, the source proof behind DRAINING/QUARANTINED and SETTLED includes each step below.

  1. Withdraw master output, channels and timer interrupt sources. Prove CEN0 before selecting the quiet enable-based trigger route; a refused CEN clear must not create a new rising TRGO2 edge.

  2. Request ADC stop, observe ADSTART/ADSTP withdrawal, disable ADC and observe ADEN/ADDIS withdrawal. A service call uses bounded progress polls outside a long interrupt-masked wait.

  3. Disable DMA and prove its state, detach both hardware addresses and count, and detach the DMAMUX route. A remaining-count snapshot does not establish a valid prefix after cancellation or error.

  4. Preserve every captured TE/OVR/break fact before acknowledging its source. Clear only observed owned DMA flags, then recheck faults. Late flags during drain must not turn into successful acquisition.

  5. Disable and join both vectors, including pending and active publishers. Under the final short metadata guard, sample faults again, detach the active request and release-publish SETTLED as the final provider access.

DMA TC alone proves none of the output, trigger or ADC withdrawal. Limited DMA prevents repeated user-buffer overwrites while the terminal IRQ is late, but does not stop TIM1. COMP break withdraws MOE; it does not stop CEN, TRGO2, ADC or DMA. These independent facts must not substitute for one another.

An ordinary successful result reports exactly the requested sample count. Any error or cancellation reports zero guaranteed valid samples. The first error, cancellation or timeout cause remains authoritative while failed withdrawal retries retain ownership. DMA completion does not override a physical fault. An incomplete stop keeps the request QUARANTINED and the private recovery state live; a later successful retry can settle it. A latched fault requires cold recovery and never automatically restarts output.

Public stop closes further admission and attempts source drain. Its success does not release pads, clocks or private storage. Platform close additionally joins both vectors, closes the subordinate hardware state, restores the original logical priorities, and only then restores comparator, pins and clocks. A failed close preserves every unfinished outer lease for the same owner to retry. Stopped identities must not be treated as revoked pointers.

Sources and software evidence¶

The authoritative public contracts are the coupled request API and the core request lifecycle. The private provider reuses the selected official ADC/DMAMUX primitives and subordinate cold helpers; it does not replace the existing TIM6 stream’s timer pointer or expose two hardware owners.

The reviewed hardware references are RM0440 Rev9, DS12288 Rev6 and ES0430 Rev9. The original official driver identities, paired CMSIS and licenses remain in the exact source lock. ADC priming, limited DMA and specific flag acknowledgement address the selected errata conditions; they are not a claim that every silicon limitation or physical circuit has been qualified.

The external register/event tests exercise real provider code, rejected aliases, priming, late DMA completion, conservative deadlines, independent refused withdrawal, final fault snapshots and late IRQ reuse. The generated-factory tests execute the normal TOML bundle, cold clock/pin/comparator construction, two-prime acquisition and retained cleanup/priority restoration. GoogleMock observes external effects; it does not replace provider methods.

These actual host executions establish their recorded software scope. The normal fixture-io-evolution-g474-pwm-adc ARM selection also links with its resource budget and retention checks: one selected face, one concrete table and five methods. That software link does not establish a clean release candidate, reproducibility or physical HIL; each requires its own exact artifact and source evidence. No inspected PCB, analog accuracy, waveform measurement, interrupt latency or continuous/simultaneous/injected acquisition is established by this finite software reference.
