Mainstream MCU adaptation and feedback¶

Use this page to extend an exact STM32 or GD32 part without weakening the common platform. The current iteration adds STM32F103RBT6 and GD32F303RCT6 reference adaptations and extends selected G474, H743 and U575 modes. Implemented source is separate from a passed software candidate. Every applicable physical result remains not_executed until connected equipment executes its station plan.

FIG-01 is the current responsibility diagram, not a claim that every peripheral or family is adapted. Its arrows express dependencies: products consume common interfaces; private SoC providers consume reviewed vendor operations. FIG-03 in the platform explanation describes TOML, facts, immutable IR, atomic generation and CMake selection. No additional runtime registry, default worker or mandatory OS layer is introduced here.

CAN, USB, Ethernet, DAC and storage/protocol services are separate capabilities; the nine-class table does not include or qualify them. Unsupported controllers, routes, transfer modes and backends must fail during resolution. They must not fall back from DMA to polling or substitute a different controller or pin. The derived capability matrix and the existing SoC ledger retain their actual authority.

The optional Ethernet contracts also remain distinct. The original eth face uses contiguous untagged frames and four-word descriptors. The separate eth_frames selection uses eight-word descriptors, scatter-gather frame loans, one in-band VLAN C-tag and MAC hardware-event timestamps on exact F407/F470 RMII references. They cannot own the same MAC simultaneously. Neither contract includes a protocol stack or network clock synchronization, and software composition does not qualify PHY/PCB wiring.

H743 SPI4 DMA selects DEV_ID 0x450, REV_ID 0x2003 (V), fixed HSI64/default trim, Cache off/on and baremetal. Its normal finite full-duplex requests borrow non-overlapping TX/RX spans in selected initialized AXI SRAM. Cache off accepts 1..65535 bytes; Cache on requires exclusive complete 32-byte lines with aligned addresses and lengths of 32..65504 bytes. DMA1 Stream2/3 remain separate from optional USART3 Stream0 TX or Stream1 RX. PLL384, FreeRTOS, 16-bit, fixed dummy/discard and multisegment SPI DMA are outside that mode. See the H743 provider contract.

The separate H743 rx-dma-continuous mode uses USART3, DMA1 Stream1/request45, DBM/CIRC and at least three equal caller-owned AXI SRAM buffers. It selects the V revision, fixed HSI64, baremetal and Cache off/on. Task service replaces the inactive bank before a conservative BRR/TIM2-derived deadline and performs Cache maintenance outside the IRQ. A late or uncertain replacement, cache failure or no FREE buffer stops production with visible loss; loans remain until actual drain. IDLE closes this producer and publishes its drained prefix; a caller must explicitly reinitialize the stream before restarting. TX remains finite IRQ, not DMA duplex. Cache-on buffers own complete aligned 32-byte lines. The mode accepts 32..65535-byte Cache-off buffers or 32..65504-byte Cache-on buffers. It does not establish measured throughput, deadline or physical qualification.

U575 USART1 dma-tx selects TZEN0, MSIS4, 8N1, Cache off and finite nonsecure SRAM1 payloads through GPDMA1 channel0/request25. IRQ-byte RX remains independent. The GPDMA contract preserves ES0499 persistent DMAT, separate UART wire TC and full source/IRQ/publisher drain. The independent irq-fifo-bytes selection has explicit thresholds and per-IRQ RX/TX byte budgets; it does not combine with DMA. These selections do not add USART2 DMA/FIFO, RX DMA, LPDMA, TrustZone transport or autonomous low-power execution.

The separate U575 dma-chain-tx mode uses the same USART1/GPDMA channel and independent IRQ-byte RX. One request borrows a contiguous SRAM1 payload, up to three blocks of at most 65535 bytes, and at most two packed 12-byte private nodes. The 24-byte node array is 32-byte aligned within one CLBAR window. The 196605-byte protocol limit also requires actual reachable, nonaliasing RAM; it does not reserve the whole SRAM for the caller. Payload and nodes remain live until fetch/channel/IRQ drain and independently observed UART TC. This finite selection does not enable scatter-gather or cyclic chains.

The independent pinless U575 LPTIM2 irq-counter16-oneshot reference selects TZEN0, MSIS4, baremetal and PCLK1/4 for a nominal 1 MHz RUN counter. Requests accept 2..65536 ticks; one tick is rejected because ARR must exceed reset CCR0. IRQ68 priority5 never calls the OS. It owns no external pin or DMA and does not promise Stop, backup or VBAT continuity. PCB and silicon revision remain unobserved; software model and link checks do not measure physical timing.

Exact mode boundaries¶

G474 has 52 GPIO pads in the RE package, not six complete sixteen-pin banks. The fixed HSE profile reserves PF0/PF1; PG10 remains reserved because its NRST option is not changed. The GPIO inventory is independent of a Board connector route. SPI1/2 use eight-bit transfers, modes 0..3, explicit active-low CS and at most 256 bytes per finite call. The 170 MHz profile caps requested SCK at 42.5 MHz: divider two would exceed the datasheet limit. G474 also implements TIM3 CH1 PA6 AF2 and TIM4 CH1 PB6 AF2 as independent whole-controller PWM owners: fixed upcounting active-high base, integer clock divisor 1..65536, period 1..65535 and staged duty 0..period. Capture, complementary outputs, break, IRQ, DMA and shared timer channels remain outside this increment.

Those PWM limits describe counter arithmetic, not unconditional pad frequency. SPI/PWM use OSPEEDR=11. DS12288 Table56 at 30 pF limits pad toggling to 50 MHz at VDD 1.62..2.7 V and 120 MHz at 2.7..3.6 V; the logical 85 MHz waveform at period two is therefore conditional. The current profile does not establish GPIO supply, load or pulse suitability; Board qualification must do so.

Polling SPI returns only after wire drain and inactive CS are proved. If a post-admission reset or CS withdrawal cannot prove that state, the executor retains the caller buffers and controller/CS owners, masks interrupts and calls the platform fault observer. It parks if the observer returns; external product recovery must not return into released storage. Idle stop/recovery failures retain their separate retryable private leases.

H743 USART3 uses PD8/PD9 AF7 and normal, finite RX DMA blocks on DMA1 Stream1, DMAMUX1 channel 1 request 45. The owner services completion and rearms the next block; gaps are possible. This is not a circular or continuous receiver. Only the selected initialized AXI/SRAM1 domains are admitted. Cache-off blocks contain 1..65535 bytes. Cache-on blocks own complete, exclusive 32-byte cache lines, with aligned address and length and a maximum length of 65504 bytes. Source withdrawal, stable remaining count and post-invalidation precede publication. A borrowed consumer or failed detach retains the stream, cache and platform dependencies.

U575 adds USART2 PA2/PA3 AF7 and package-valid GPIO routes. PG[15:2] require an already established VDDIO2 supply and observed PWR clock/IO2SV configuration; the platform does not enable an unknown external rail. IO2SV is a configuration observation, not a measurement of rail voltage. TZEN0 remains the general platform profile. The separate TZEN1 reference also accepts cooperative GPIO jobs of at most eight PC7 commands copied into one Secure slot. SERVICE advances at most one command, POLL reads latched status, CANCEL preserves earlier effects and CONSUME releases only a terminal job. Tickets never wrap; no NS address survives a call. This provides neither a user-buffer loan nor general asynchronous or cross-security transport support.

F103 standalone admission checks the CPU-readable factory Flash density, not a debug-only device-group or revision value. ES096 Rev15 section2.2.2 reports zero from normal-software DBGMCU reads; group and revision therefore remain unobserved. The configured STM32F103RBT6 declaration and package still require separate integration evidence. See the F103 source boundary.

F103 uses USART2 PA2/PA3 and PA0/PA1 EXTI events. F103 SPI1 and GD303 SPI0 use default PA5/PA6/PA7 with explicit PA4 active-low CS, nominal 31.25 kHz..4 MHz, eight-bit words, modes 0..3 and at most 256 bytes per finite call. Their SPI assemblies cannot also select the PA5 GPIO/LED binding. F103 TIM2 is 16-bit at a nominal 1 MHz; its overflow service and mask interval must stay below 65.536 ms. GD303 cascades TIMER1 update TRGO into TIMER4 ITI0 and extends the high overflow, with a service interval below 2^32 nominal microseconds. This preserves low wraps across the stated 800 ms Flash erase bound; the timer-bridge carry propagation still needs physical observation. Neither profile establishes calibrated wall time or FreeRTOS tick catch-up after same-bank fetch stalls.

Mode resources, acquisition and physical Flash¶

F103 USART2 uses DMA1 channel7 for TX and channel6 for finite RX; GD303 USART0 uses DMA0 channel3/channel4. TX-only construction has the compact TX state and explicit IRQ-byte ring; duplex alone adds caller-supplied RX blocks. Both admit only declared SRAM spans of 1..65535 bytes. Memory TC does not settle TX before wire TC and source withdrawal. Finite RX publishes only after channel/DMAR withdrawal and stable count; owner service rearms a free block, so gaps and visible loss remain part of the contract. An unavailable replacement never authorizes overwriting a consumer loan. Their PB6/PB7 I2C uses priority-zero IRQ handling and rejects kernel notifications; bus voltage and pull-ups are external. The shared legacy engine remains the sole protocol owner.

G474 ADC1 selects single-ended 12-bit bulb sampling, SYSCLK/128 = 1.328125 MHz and sample code0. Software shots discard the first conversion and keep their explicit warmup and spacing guards. TIM6 finite normal-DMA blocks accept only 2.5–50 kHz rates that divide the 1 MHz trigger timebase exactly. Bulb aperture depends on the preceding EOC and next trigger, not SMPR alone. IRQ stops the trigger; service observes DMA/ADC withdrawal and ADEN clear before publication, then a later service rearms. The admitted SRAM1/2 domain excludes CCM aliases even though silicon can DMA through its 0x20018000 alias. The 32 KiB CCM counts once. See the exact G474 contract for errata and aperture bounds.

H743’s optional D2 SRAM1 has explicit budgeting, gate admission and startup DATA/BSS initialization; NOINIT stays caller-owned. DMA rechecks initialized AXI/SRAM1 and clock state before borrowing. H743 ADC1 provides 16-bit raw shots on PA3/INP15 with cut-specific boost and complete linearity calibration; U575 ADC4 provides 12-bit PC0/channel1 shots after external VDDA/ASV admission. F103/GD303 use 12-bit PA0/channel0 shots at 4 MHz, with part-specific warmup and calibration. These formats and declared reference voltages are not measured analog accuracy. Their exact selected PWM/EXTI routes and pin conflicts remain in support and the adjacent SoC contracts.

Physical Flash exposes the whole exact part, not a product partition. Authored supply_mv is a mandatory software assumption where required, never a voltage measurement. nx_flash_geometry_t.program_flags can set NX_FLASH_PROGRAM_REQUIRES_FRESH_ERASE: each unit needs a successful erase and no later programming pulse. All-FF readback cannot prove hidden ECC history; failed/interrupted pulses consume the unit until its next successful erase. Providers keep no implicit history bitmap. Zero flags do not waive a provider’s own programming rules, and all-FF input units issue no pulse.

Every physical pulse first clears and observes stale error/completion state, then requires fresh completion after its actual busy/write-buffer drain. G474, U575 and F407 gate EOP latching with EOPIE; GD470 uses ENDIE for its end flag. Their polling owner exclusively leases a disabled/inactive FLASH IRQ while selected pulse interrupt bits are temporarily set; the NVIC never enables that vector. Cleanup removes pulse mode/enables, locks and observes pending withdrawal while preserving priority. Legacy F103/GD303 and H743 completion flags latch unconditionally under their register contracts; the interrupt-enable precondition is not inferred for them. H743 drains BSY/QW/WBNE and requires Cache off; U575 drains BSY/WDW in TZEN0/SWAP0.

Timeout prevents a later pulse but cannot abort one already started. Same-bank fetch/IRQ can stall, and a permanently busy controller never returns caller storage as completed. Product code owns writable erase-aligned regions, voltage, watchdog, execution placement and power-loss recovery. The common Flash adapter does not invent erase history. The maintained two-bank storage algorithm erases the inactive bank before a save, writes distinct full header/payload units and programs a separate commit unit last; a failed save requires reopen/recovery before a later save erases that target again.

Watchdog activation is irreversible, including a programming failure after the start command. Preparation failures retain any unwithdrawn LSI or debug lease for stop retry. Programmed nominal duration is distinct from physical minimum and maximum duration. GD303 and F470 datasheets provide nominal internal RC frequency without a reviewed min/max envelope: successful programming returns success with zero bounds; an enabled state query reports unsupported bounds while preserving enabled, irreversible and actual debug facts. U575 rejects prescaled LSI and requires a nominal minimum of 375 microseconds so RLR is at least two. Bounds from every other part retain their own datasheet conditions.

Adaptation must improve the shared platform¶

For every new part, first identify exact differences: register generation, clock/reset domains, interrupt topology, DMA routing, cache reachability, package pins, errata and irreversible effects. Put those facts in the SoC description and reviewed routes. Keep PCB electrical facts in Board data and product policy in external repositories. A compiler define cannot substitute for this review.

Use an existing provider only where its hardware and ownership assumptions hold. Extract a common mechanism after two concrete adaptations demonstrate identical semantics and failure ordering. Prefer compile-time helpers or shared production algorithms with private hardware ports; avoid forwarding-only runtime layers, family-name condition chains and descriptors that merely duplicate generated constants. Measure code, RAM and stack before accepting a generalization.

The new legacy GPIO adaptations should feed back package validation, atomic set/reset, output-before-mode ordering and remap conflict handling. Their UARTs should feed back clock rounding, live-device rejection, IRQ mask restoration, error/data ordering and late-interrupt isolation. F103 TIM2 is 16-bit, unlike the F407 32-bit TIM2: an overflow extension requires an explicit maximum service gap and cannot silently reuse the longer wrap assumption.

This iteration applies that feedback to timer admission and cleanup: recheck retained slave, channel and trigger state after observing the timer gate; keep a gate-only lease separate from an admitted timer; and select a quiet trigger source while issuing the prescaler update. A refused reset, trigger restore or gate withdrawal retains the corresponding cleanup responsibility. Watchdog reviews likewise propagate status-safe observation, conservative mixed shadow update bounds and retryable preparation cleanup without assuming identical RC or reload semantics across vendors.

Actual library behavior must be reviewed, including hidden effects: GD32F4’s combined fwdgt_config starts the irreversible counter before programming. Treating that call as reversible preparation is invalid. Likewise, new M3 images use soft-float while existing M4F images use hard-float. Resource, retention, candidate and reproducibility tools obtain ABI from the sole resolved CPU contract and still validate ELF flags, linked configuration and input bytes; they never infer ABI from an artifact to make it pass.

Official LL helpers are reviewed operations, not automatically bounded code. For example, F1 ATOMIC_* USART setters may contain exclusive-store retry loops. Use bounded register operations inside the provider’s existing saved-mask region where required; keep safe LL data/flag operations private. Errata review must consider the selected mode and silicon revision, including GD32F30x USART DMA enable ordering, timer capture DMA/shadow interaction and ADC clock constraints.

Acceptance sequence and next admissions¶

Each adaptation follows the same sequence: source/license review; failing configuration and real-provider tests; minimal implementation; passing tests; strict resource/mode rejection; ARM image/resource checks; relocated SDK consumption; and clean-source candidate qualification. Preserve the actual RED failure and GREEN commands. GoogleTest/GoogleMock remain host-only; firmware remains C11 and allocation-free by default. The qualification contract defines the evidence requirements rather than the number of files or checks in this page.

Exercise partial start failure, retry, reverse cleanup, instance isolation, accepted cancellation, late IRQ and failed drain wherever the selected mode can reach them. A failed drain retains QUARANTINED storage; a timeout never revokes the borrow. Only acquire-observed SETTLED, after required references detach, ends the provider’s access. The caller still drains its observers and controls.

The physical station separately observes chip/revision, Board/bridges, clock, image, reset and I/O behavior. Performance statements require the same image, clock, workload and instrument. Host test time and operation counts are not MCU cycles, IRQ latency, electrical correctness or safety qualification. Record hardware-dependent exits as pending while no equipment is connected.

Further mainstream candidates include STM32F072RBT6, STM32F303RET6, STM32G071RBT6, STM32L476RGT6 and GD32E230F8P6. These are proposed admission targets, not supported parts. Confirm official package/memory facts, source licenses, toolchain/ABI, a finite peripheral set and a separately sourced Board before implementation. Other GD32F4xx and STM32F4 parts likewise need explicit variant facts and routes; neither an SDK header nor matching core grants support.

Bootloader handoff, runtime reclocking, deep STOP, generic cross-domain DMA and broader U575 asynchronous transport retain their separate exits in the SoC refinement ledger. This iteration does not close those tasks by adding an MCU family or linking an official library.
