# OS development regression evidence

`manifest.json` binds the unchanged bytes of executed RED and later GREEN logs
to their original paths and SHA-256 identities. Case sets can grow between runs.
The records cover meaningful contracts and regressions, not every individual edit.

These are development records. They do not qualify the final source, establish
the historical order of all changes, certify a product or imply physical HIL.
Final validation independently executes the complete test plan on clean source.
Hardware remains `not_executed` until an admitted station runs real equipment.
